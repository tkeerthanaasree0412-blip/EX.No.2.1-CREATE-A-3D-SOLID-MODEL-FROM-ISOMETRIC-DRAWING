# EX.No.2.1  CREATE A 3D SOLID MODEL FROM ISOMETRIC DRAWING
## Date:

## AIM
To create a 3Dmodel from the given isometric drawing using fusion 360 tool. 

## REQUIREMENTS
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE

1.Select Sketch > Create Sketch.

2. Select the Front (XZ) Plane

3.Select Sketch > Rectangle > Center Rectangle.
<img width="363" height="461" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ab45a672-3951-45bb-b5ef-f02a4ed476c9" />

4.Now hover over the origin (center) of the sketch. You should see the cursor snap to this location.

5.Click once to begin placing the rectangle.

6.Click again to place the rectangle.

7.Use dimensions to enter 100 mm for width and 50mm for height of the rectangle

8.Click Finish Sketch after completing.
<img width="201" height="170" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-11 174217" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/734ac8c9-8136-48c3-b5fa-c1683af400ee" />


9.Select Extrude and select the Rectangle sketch.
<img width="503" height="605" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-03 211338" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4073a8ec-227c-4107-ba29-bf1421d740e7" />


10.Type in the value of 30mm in the dimension table that is opened and press ENTER to accept the value.

11. Now Select Sketch and then go to  Create Sketch.Select the front surface of rectangular block

12.After that Select Sketch and then circle and choose the center circle option
<img width="363" height="461" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/244c710f-a6c3-4d5b-9543-9a497f240ec3" />

13.Now hover over the origin (center) of the sketch.Click the cursor at thr center and enter the diameter of the circle as 50mm.Now the circle is drawn

14.Click Finish Sketch after completing.

15.Select Extrude.Select the circle sketch.Enter 20mm in the dimensions table and the press enter

16.Now select create sketch again and select the top surface of the circle

17.Select rectangle and draw a rectangle with dimensions 30mm x 30mm at the center 

18.Press finish sketch and select the extrude option and enter 10mm as dimension and extrude it

19.Select the required edges of the extruded block and apply the Chamfer command.Set the chamfer angle to 45° to obtain the sloping surface as shown in the given figure.
<img width="527" height="573" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-05 224224" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/092769c2-fb95-4b24-9217-15ea987eb804" />

20.Select the top surface on the right side and create a new sketch.Draw a circle of 20 mm diameter (Ø20) at the specified position.Finish the sketch and extrude it by 10 mm.

21.Select the top-left surface and start a new sketch.Draw a square of 15 mm × 15 mm using the Rectangle tool.Position it according to the given offsets of 15 mm and 12 mm.Finish the sketch and extrude it by 10 mm.

22.Complete the 3D model and save the design.Select Design → Drawing → From Design.
<img width="284" height="453" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-11 172436" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bdd3c885-03b4-406b-a390-ab4d83b96a92" />
<img width="415" height="462" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-11 172509" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a76e1fa1-d3e9-4f02-b1cc-59c60129e578" />

23.Insert the Front, Top, Side and Isometric views.Add the necessary dimensions and save the drawing
